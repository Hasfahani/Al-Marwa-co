Machinery & Large Cargo Shipping for Businesses

Shipping production equipment, a large tank, or an industrial machine starts with a practical loading plan. AL MARWA CO focuses on large commercial cargo, FCL worldwide, and full truckloads on our seven core lanes. We review the transport dimensions, weight, lifting needs, and site access before confirming a freight option.

Review the transport unit, not just the product

A machine’s working dimensions may differ from its transport dimensions. Tell us whether it will be dismantled, crated, secured to a base, or moved as one unit. List any loose attachments separately. The lifting points, transport orientation, and weight of each piece help establish how it can be loaded.

For a tank or equipment previously used with liquids or other materials, describe its previous contents and current condition. Acceptance and any additional handling requirements must be reviewed before booking. Photographs and drawings help us understand the cargo; they do not replace the final packing and weight information.

FCL can be practical for one large commercial item

A dedicated container does not have to be completely full. One large item can be reviewed as an FCL shipment when its size, weight, loading method, and route make that practical. This is how we assess large business cargo from origins outside our main import markets as well.

If the item does not fit standard container handling, we assess whether a suitable equipment and handling plan is available. The quote sets out the container or vehicle, lifting, loading and delivery scope we have agreed with you.

What to send for a machinery freight review

  • Length, width, height and gross weight of every transportable piece.
  • Current photographs, packaging plan, drawings and identified lifting points.
  • Whether the equipment can be tilted, stacked or dismantled.
  • Pickup and delivery access, including clearances and ground-level handling needs.
  • Who provides dismantling, packing, crane work, loading and unloading.
  • Cargo readiness, project deadline and the company receiving the equipment.

Business freight questions

Can you ship one large tank or machine from another country?

We can review it as a business FCL shipment, subject to container fit, loading feasibility, cargo acceptance, and route availability. Send its dimensions, weight, condition, photographs, and pickup details.

Do you guarantee that oversized equipment fits a container?

No. Fit and loading feasibility are assessed using the actual transport dimensions, weight, lifting arrangements, and available equipment. A proposed option is confirmed only after that review.

What details matter for a used tank?

Describe its previous contents, present condition, transport dimensions, weight, packaging, and lifting points. Any acceptance and handling requirements are confirmed before booking.

Can the quote include lifting and site handling?

Tell us which lifting, loading, unloading, or placement tasks you need. We review site access and equipment requirements, then define which tasks can be included in the agreed scope.

Plan the commercial load

Send a shipment brief your team can work from

Share your company, route, container or truck count, packed dimensions, gross weight, readiness date, and requested pickup and delivery scope. For large equipment, include photographs and loading details.

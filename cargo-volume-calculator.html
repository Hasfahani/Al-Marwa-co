Calculated volume is not a loading plan, container-fit check or freight price. The packing arrangement, openings, weight and handling still need review.



How cubic volume is calculated
For each rectangular package type, convert the external length, width and height to metres, multiply those three measurements, then multiply by the quantity. Add the package-type totals together. For centimetres, divide each dimension by 100 before multiplying.


For example, four packed units measuring 120 × 80 × 100 cm have a total volume of 3.84 m³. If each weighs 250 kg gross, their total gross weight is 1,000 kg. This calculation describes those packages; it does not prove how they can be arranged inside a container.


Volume is one part of the loading review
Measure crates, pallets, protective frames and protruding parts as they will travel. Irregular machinery may need a transport envelope that includes space which cannot be filled with another package. Provide photographs and explain orientation or stacking restrictions.


Do not subtract empty spaces inside equipment from its external transport dimensions. Container openings, loading access, usable floor space, weight distribution and lifting arrangements require a separate operational review. Our specialty is FCL worldwide and large B2B loads; LCL is limited to the selected origins into Lebanon.

                    
Business freight questions
What does CBM mean?
CBM means cubic metres, a measure of volume. For rectangular packed goods it is calculated from length in metres multiplied by width in metres, height in metres and quantity.
Should we measure the goods or their packaging?
Use external dimensions in transport condition, including pallets, crates, frames and protruding parts. Send separate rows for different package sizes.
Does the calculator estimate a freight price?
No. It measures packed volume and sums supplied gross weights. A freight price needs the route, cargo, dates, handling requirements and requested service scope.
Are calculator details sent to AL MARWA CO automatically?
No. Calculations run in your browser. Choosing the quote transfer keeps a temporary draft in your browser and opens the contact form. Information is sent only when you submit an inquiry through the form or contact a service yourself.

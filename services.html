B2B Freight: FCL Worldwide, Full Truckloads & Large Cargo

Our main focus is B2B freight: worldwide FCL (Full Container Load), full truckloads (FTL) from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom and China, and large commercial cargo. LCL (Less than Container Load) is available from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and China into Lebanon, subject to shipment and route confirmation. Land Freight

Full truckload (FTL) transport for substantial commercial cargo from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom and China.

AL MARWA CO focuses on full truckloads for importers, exporters, manufacturers, and project teams. Our key markets include Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and China; road legs and truckload feasibility are confirmed for each route.

Core Capabilities

  • Full truckload (FTL) planning for business cargo
  • 18-meter trailer operations
  • Bulk and palletized freight
  • Door-to-door coordination
  • Cross-border movement support

Heavy Lifting

Controlled handling for oversized and industrial cargo requiring specialized equipment and rigging.

Our team handles industrial machinery and oversized cargo with the technical planning needed for lifting, loading, and final placement.

Core Capabilities

  • Cranes and rigging team support
  • Industrial equipment transportation
  • Low-bed trailer deployment
  • On-site loading and unloading coordination

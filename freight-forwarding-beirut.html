What We Check Before Beirut Cargo Moves

A fast quote is useful only when it reflects the real shipment. We review the cargo profile, timing, documents, pickup access, terminal requirements, and delivery constraints that can change the cost or schedule.

  • Matching cargo urgency with the right transport mode
  • Preparing paperwork before dispatch to avoid hold-ups

  • Communicating realistic milestones to the shipper or consignee

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve companies located inside Beirut?

Yes. We coordinate freight for businesses in Beirut and Greater Beirut from our Khalde office, including local pickup and delivery requirements where included in the shipment scope.

Can you coordinate both port and airport cargo?

Yes. Depending on the shipment, we can plan sea freight, air cargo, road transport, and the related customs and delivery coordination.

What information speeds up a quote?

Send the origin, destination, cargo description, quantity, dimensions, weight, target timing, and whether pickup, customs support, or delivery is needed.

Privacy Policy

Plain-language information about the details collected through this website and how they are used for freight inquiries.

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Who is responsible for your information?

AL MARWA CO, Khalde Highway, Lebanon, is responsible for information submitted through this website. Privacy questions can be sent to info@al-marwaco.com or discussed by phone at +961 70 099978.

What information do we collect?

When you submit a quote or contact form, you may provide:

  • Your name, company, email address, and telephone number
  • Shipment origin, destination, freight type, weight, and timing
  • Your message and any operational details you choose to include

Please do not include passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or unrelated sensitive personal information in a shipment inquiry.

Why do we use this information?

We use submitted information to review your request, prepare or discuss a freight quote, contact you about the shipment, and maintain necessary business correspondence. We do not use the inquiry forms to subscribe you to marketing messages.

Form processing

The website forms are processed by FormSubmit and delivered to our company email address. Information submitted through those forms is therefore transmitted through FormSubmit's systems. You can review the provider's own privacy information on the FormSubmit privacy page.

WhatsApp, maps, and external websites

If you choose a WhatsApp, Google Maps, carrier, or other external link, that service processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control those third-party websites.

Retention and sharing

Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage related business communication, meet record-keeping obligations, or resolve a dispute. We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or operational partners only when needed to process the inquiry or arrange an agreed shipment.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion where continued retention is not required. Email info@al-marwaco.com and provide enough detail for us to identify the relevant inquiry.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website, form process, or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this policy.

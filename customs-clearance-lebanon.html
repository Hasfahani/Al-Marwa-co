Documentation and Customs

Customs Coordination in Lebanon for B2B Freight

AL MARWA CO supports customs readiness in Lebanon through shipment document review and customs-clearance coordination. Required documents and the service scope are confirmed for each cargo and route; clearance decisions remain with the authorities.

Why Customs Preparation Matters

Fewer Avoidable Delays

  • Incomplete details slow shipment movement
  • Late document review creates risk
  • Early checks improve execution readiness

Clearer Shipment Information

  • Goods description matters
  • Weights and dimensions should align
  • Origin and destination details should be ready

Better Coordination

  • Documents affect carrier planning
  • Customs readiness shapes route choices
  • One workflow helps reduce miscommunication

Useful Information to Prepare Early

Every shipment differs, but having the basics ready early makes the planning process smoother and helps reduce unnecessary back-and-forth.

  • Accurate cargo description
  • Origin, destination, and consignee details
  • Dimensions, weight, and quantity
  • Commercial invoice and supporting shipment information
  • Special handling notes or known customs constraints

Frequently Asked Questions

Can customs issues delay both air and sea shipments?

Yes. Documentation readiness can affect any freight mode if shipment information is incomplete or inconsistent.

Do we need all shipment details before asking for help?

No. You can start with the route, cargo type, and what you already know. From there, we can help identify what is still needed.

Is customs support separate from freight forwarding?

It is closely connected. Good freight forwarding usually includes documentation-aware planning because the shipment route depends on it.

Need to Prepare a Shipment Properly?

Share your cargo details and route. We will help identify the key documentation and shipping steps before delays start.

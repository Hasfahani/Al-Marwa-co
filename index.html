Freight Answers

Before You Book a Shipment

Clear answers to the practical questions businesses ask before moving cargo through Beirut and Lebanon.

What details are needed for a freight quote?

Share the origin, destination, cargo description, package count, dimensions, weight, preferred timing, and whether pickup, customs support, or final delivery is required. Photos and a packing list help with unusual cargo.

Should I choose air freight or sea freight?

Air freight generally suits urgent, compact, or higher-value cargo. Sea freight is usually more practical for containers, larger volumes, and flexible timelines. We compare the route, schedule, handling, and total requirements before recommending an option.

Can you coordinate customs support and delivery?

Yes. Planning can include document review, customs-clearance coordination, handling, and delivery requirements. The exact scope and required documents are confirmed for each shipment.

Do you serve businesses in Beirut?

Yes. From our Khalde office, we coordinate freight for Beirut, Greater Beirut, and businesses across Lebanon, including port and airport movements when appropriate for the route.

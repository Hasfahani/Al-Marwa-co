Match the freight service to the direction of travel

Business freight questions

What are your main import routes into Lebanon?

Our main import markets are Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and China. We focus on B2B FCL and large commercial loads, with LCL from these origins into Lebanon and full truckloads on our seven core lanes.

Can a business ship FCL from another country?

Yes. Worldwide FCL is our specialty. Send the origin, destination, cargo dimensions, weight, loading needs, and readiness date so we can review the route and total service scope.

Do you run full truckloads on these routes?

Yes. We run full truckloads by road from all seven markets into Lebanon, alongside FCL. For each shipment we confirm routing, vehicle, schedule and price before booking.

Do you also ship from Lebanon to other countries?

Yes. We coordinate FCL exports from Lebanon worldwide. Destination acceptance, route, schedule, and the requested pickup and delivery scope are confirmed for each shipment.

Keep the service scope clear

FCL worldwide is our specialty for commercial imports to Lebanon and exports from Lebanon. Full truckloads run on our seven core lanes. LCL is offered only from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom and China into Lebanon, subject to shipment and route confirmation.

A calculator result does not establish container fit or equipment selection. Door openings, gross weight, loading method, orientation and usable space must also be reviewed for the actual shipment. Send the complete packing list rather than only a total volume.

Business freight questions

Do the planning tools provide a freight price?

No. The planner identifies the service to review, and the calculator measures packed cargo volume. A freight quote needs the actual route, cargo, dates and requested service scope.

Can we download a quote checklist?

Yes. The B2B quote checklist is available as a text download on this page. It covers company details, the route, cargo, loading units, readiness and requested responsibilities.

Does a CBM total confirm that cargo fits a container?

No. Usable space, openings, orientation, gross weight, packaging and loading method must also be checked. Provide an itemized packing list for review.

B2B Sea Freight in Lebanon: FCL Worldwide

FCL (Full Container Load) worldwide is our specialty. We coordinate full containers for importers, exporters, manufacturers, and businesses moving large cargo to or from Lebanon. LCL (Less than Container Load) is available from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and China into Lebanon, subject to shipment and route confirmation.

When Sea Freight Is a Good Fit

Sea freight works best when the shipment profile supports longer transit windows in exchange for better load flexibility, more practical container planning, or lower cost pressure than air cargo.

  • Containerized commercial cargo
  • Larger loads where air freight is not practical
  • Shipments that can work with planned transit schedules
  • Routes that require coordinated port and inland handling
  • Imports and exports needing documentation-aware support

Questions to Prepare Before Requesting a Quote

  • Origin and destination
  • Container requirement or cargo dimensions
  • Weight and goods description
  • Required shipping timeline
  • Any customs or consignee constraints

FCL, LCL or a Truckload: Start With the Business Load

Full Container Load means dedicated container use. A shipment does not have to occupy every cubic metre to be reviewed as FCL. For machinery, large items and recurring commercial orders, container fit, loading access and the complete service scope matter as much as volume.

  • FCL worldwide: our main specialty for substantial business imports to Lebanon and exports from Lebanon.
  • Selected-origin LCL: smaller commercial imports only from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom and China into Lebanon.
  • Full truckloads: by road from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom and China into Lebanon.

Compare the same pickup and delivery points and identify the handling tasks included. A volume total or one freight-rate line does not establish the complete loading plan or shipment cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries do you offer LCL imports from?

LCL (Less than Container Load) is available from Ukraine, Jordan, Iraq, the UAE, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and China into Lebanon, subject to shipment and route confirmation.

Do you specialize in FCL worldwide?

Yes. FCL (Full Container Load) worldwide is our specialty for commercial imports to Lebanon and exports from Lebanon. Machinery, equipment, and bulky items are reviewed for container fit, loading requirements, and route availability.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL refers to a full container load, while LCL refers to less-than-container load shipping for smaller cargo volumes.

Is sea freight always cheaper than air freight?

Sea freight is often more cost-efficient for larger loads, but the right choice still depends on urgency, cargo profile, and total route requirements.

Can you coordinate sea freight with inland transport too?

Yes. Many routes need sea freight combined with road transport and documentation support for smoother end-to-end execution.

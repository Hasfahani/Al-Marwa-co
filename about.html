About AL MARWA CO

Our Company

Founded in 1997, AL MARWA CO has grown into one of the region’s trusted names in forwarding, shipping, and trading. With more than 25 years of hands-on logistics experience, we support businesses across Lebanon, the Middle East, Asia, and Europe.

Our philosophy is simple: We listen. We adapt. We deliver. Through personalized solutions, transparent communication, and commitment to reliability, we have built long-term partnerships with clients worldwide.

About AL MARWA CO

Logistics That Moves With You

Building reliable freight partnerships across the region since 1997.

Our Company

Founded in 1997, AL MARWA CO has built a steady record in freight forwarding, shipping, and logistics coordination across the Middle East, Europe, and Asia.

With over 29 years of hands-on operations experience, our team delivers professional planning, clear communication, and practical execution for clients who depend on reliable freight partnerships.

Our approach is direct: We listen to the requirement, plan the right route, and follow through to delivery.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality logistics coordination that meets client needs through integrity, operational discipline, and consistent follow-through on every shipment.

Our Vision

To be the most dependable logistics partner for businesses that need consistent, practical freight solutions across multiple transport modes and regions.

Core Values

  • Reliability and on-time performance
  • Transparent, clear communication
  • Expertise across all freight modes
  • Client-focused logistics planning

What Procurement and Supply-Chain Teams Can Define Up Front

A stronger B2B freight proposal starts with a clear operating scope. We help teams compare options on the same basis and identify the decisions that affect total handling, transit, and delivery—not only the headline freight rate.

  • Lane, frequency, shipment profile, Incoterms, and service scope
  • Required pickup, port or airport, customs, and final-delivery steps
  • Milestone updates, responsible contacts, and escalation expectations
  • Special handling for oversized, fragile, high-priority, or site-sensitive cargo

Information for a B2B Freight Proposal or RFP

Share the operational scope early so the route, service level, responsibilities, and commercial assumptions can be compared on the same basis.

  • Origin, destination, commodity, Incoterms, and required scope
  • Expected frequency, annual or project volume, dimensions, and weight
  • Required transit window, delivery deadline, or production milestone
  • Pickup, customs, final delivery, lifting, or site-access requirements
  • Reporting cadence, contact structure, and known escalation requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle both regional and international shipments from Lebanon?

Yes. We support freight planning for regional road movement as well as international sea and air cargo depending on the shipment profile and destination.

Can you help if we are not sure which mode to choose?

Yes. Many shipments start with uncertainty around cost, transit speed, or handling requirements. We help narrow the right option based on urgency, cargo type, and route practicality.

Can you support recurring B2B freight lanes?

Yes. Planning can cover repeat import or export movements, agreed shipment information, carrier coordination, document checks, milestone updates, and escalation contacts. The operating model is defined around the lane and shipment requirements.

What should our company include in an RFP or quote request?

Include the lane, commodity, volumes, frequency, dimensions and weight, Incoterms, transit window, customs scope, pickup and delivery requirements, plus any reporting or site constraints.

Planning a Corporate Freight Lane or Project?

Send the route, shipment profile, frequency, required scope, and decision timeline. We will structure a B2B freight proposal around the operational requirements.
